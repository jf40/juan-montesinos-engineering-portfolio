About

Engineering from first principles to a physical result.

I’m studying Mechanical Engineering at the University of Bath and gravitate toward projects where analysis directly shapes a real component: vehicle structures, safety-critical hardware, manufacturing and motorsport systems.

I’m particularly interested in the intersection of structural design, simulation and advanced manufacturing — taking a requirement, understanding the loads, designing the geometry, validating it and ultimately making the part.
