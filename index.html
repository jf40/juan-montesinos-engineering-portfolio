LPBF-manufactured motorcycle triple clamp

Designing an LPBF-manufactured motorcycle triple clamp around the structural constraints defined by three load scenarios: emergency braking, cornering and straight-line driving.

  • Performed topology optimisation in Ansys Mechanical to reduce mass while preserving structural stiffness.
  • Used modal analysis to identify the dominant vibration modes and high-amplitude regions of the component.
  • Investigating enclosed powder pockets as passive damping elements to improve both stiffness-to-weight performance and vibration damping.
  • Researching Ti-6Al-4V LPBF behaviour, post-processing and design constraints.
Ansys Mechanical (Topology optimisation) Autodesk Inventor

Bath Zero · MotoStudent 2027 bike

Tubular mainframe chassis

Designed and validated the tubular motorcycle mainframe, balancing packaging, stiffness, manufacturability and fatigue performance in the welded joints.

  • Developed the frame geometry in Autodesk Inventor around the motorcycle packaging and structural requirements.
  • Used Ansys Mechanical sizing optimisation to refine tube dimensions against stiffness and strength targets.
  • Assessed stress and fatigue in the weld heat-affected zones in accordance with IIW guideline XIII-1823-07.
  • Used Python to support engineering calculations and design evaluation.

About

I’m studying Mechanical Engineering at the University of Bath, with an increasing focus on structural analysis and manufacturing.

I’m particularly interested in finite element simulation, additive manufacturing and composite structures, especially where analysis can directly inform the design and manufacture of physical components.

I’m fascinated by the design freedom and material possibilities offered by composites and additive manufacturing.

I have also maintained a strong interest in computer science alongside my mechanical engineering work, with experience in Arduino-based systems, telecommunications, MATLAB and programming in Java, C#, C++ and Python. I enjoy using these tools where they strengthen the design, analysis and testing of physical engineering systems.
